## 2. What Customers Complain About

No verifiable customer complaints were retrieved for either brand in the supplied research. This means complaints are **unknown**, not that customers have none.

The material available was company-controlled praise, not complaint evidence: iBites testimonials attributed to Priya S., Rahul M., Sneha K. and Amit D. praise flavor, freshness and energy ([iBites site](https://ibites.in/)). Earth Organic Juices testimonials attributed to Preeti Dhanve, Shivlal Chavan, Saraswati Gujiri and Mrunal Bijwe refer to fresh or natural taste, no preservatives, texture and long-term amla use ([Earth Organic Juices site](https://earthorganicjuices.com/)). Independent verification of these testimonials is unknown. These positive statements should not be interpreted as evidence of customer complaints or as independently verified findings.

## 3. Market Gap

A research-visible opportunity—not proof that no provider offers these features—is to make the per-juice price and pack size explicit, and to clearly explain weekly choice or variety terms. Neither competitor has a verified unit price or pack size in the supplied research. iBites is described as delivering the same juice each morning on its daily plan; Earth Organic Juices’ weekly variety terms are unknown.

The supplied research does not establish gym-goer-specific demand or prove that competitors lack choice. Treat both as questions to validate with prospective customers.

## 4. Recommended Positioning

**One-line offer:** “A Pune cold-pressed juice subscription with clear per-juice pricing and a week of choice or planned variety.”

**Recommendation:** Test ₹699 for a seven-day plan with one juice per day (about ₹100 per juice). This is a proposed test price, not a researched market price; no competitor price is verified.

**Assumptions to validate before sale:** the plan includes seven servings; serving size and delivery terms will be specified; the business can operationally provide the promised choice or rotation; and customers accept the proposed price. Confirm service areas and delivery operations before advertising. Gym-goer-specific demand is not evidenced in the supplied research.

## 5. 30-Day Launch Plan

These are proposed actions, not claims about completed research or existing operations.

**Week 1**
- Confirm production and delivery feasibility, service areas, serving size and delivery terms.
- Decide whether customers can choose juices or receive a stated rotation; document the exact seven-day plan.
- Validate the proposed ₹699 test price with prospective customers in the target age and gym-goer segment.

**Week 2**
- Prepare a simple offer page or ordering flow that states the per-juice price, serving size, schedule, choice/rotation terms and service area.
- Invite a small test group of prospective customers to review the offer and report questions or objections.
- Adjust the plan based on operational constraints and feedback; do not advertise unconfirmed delivery coverage.

**Week 3**
- Run a limited pilot with consenting customers in confirmed service areas.
- Track orders, repeat intent, delivery reliability and feedback on juice choice and serving size.
- Record customer feedback directly; do not infer complaints or satisfaction beyond what participants report.

**Week 4**
- Review pilot feedback, delivery performance and response to the test price.
- Revise price, plan terms or choice/rotation based on observed results and feasible operations.
- Expand promotion only where service coverage and the advertised terms are confirmed.
